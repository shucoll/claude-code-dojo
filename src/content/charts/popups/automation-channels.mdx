The inverse of polling. A channel is an MCP server that pushes events into a
session you already have open, so a CI result or a message from your phone
arrives where Claude still has your files and your context.

**Next turn starts when:** an external event arrives. Nothing is on a timer.

**Machine on:** yes. **Session open:** yes, and events only arrive while it is.

Research preview. Opt in per session with `--channels plugin:name@marketplace`,
only from an approved plugin allowlist. Team and Enterprise organizations are
blocked until an Owner sets `channelsEnabled`.
