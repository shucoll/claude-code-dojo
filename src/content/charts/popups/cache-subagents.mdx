A subagent opens its own conversation with its own system prompt and tool set.
Its first request cannot read the parent's cache, because the two prefixes
differ, and it warms a cache of its own across its turns. From the parent's
side the call and its result append to the conversation, so the parent's prefix
survives intact.

Subagents sit outside the main-conversation TTL bucket, so they get five
minutes even on a subscription until you set `subagentPromptCacheTtl`.

A fork is the exception. It inherits the parent's system prompt, tools, and
history exactly, so its first request reads the parent's cache.
