A dedicated virtual machine with its own kernel, and in cloud or microVM
deployments its own virtualized hardware. Cloud instances, local hypervisors,
and microVMs such as Firecracker all qualify.

**Isolates:** a full operating system, which is the strongest separation
available and the only tier that puts a kernel boundary between the agent and
your host.

**Still outside:** whatever the VM can reach on the network, and any credential
you provisioned inside it.

This is the documented answer for evaluating untrusted code, for a security
policy that requires kernel-level separation, and for compliance requirements
no host-level approach satisfies.
