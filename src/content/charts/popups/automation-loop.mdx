Session-scoped. `/loop` re-runs a prompt while the session stays open, backed by
the `CronCreate`, `CronList`, and `CronDelete` tools.

**Next turn starts when:** a time interval elapses. With an interval you get a
cron job; without one Claude picks each delay itself, between one minute and an
hour, and prints the delay and its reason.

**Machine on:** yes. **Session open:** yes, though backgrounding the session
carries the tasks over.

Recurring tasks expire seven days after creation, firing once more before they
delete themselves. A session holds at most 50 tasks.
