A scheduled task on your own machine, created from the Desktop app's Code tab by
turning on **Only on this computer**, or by choosing **Local** where the app
offers **Local** and **Cloud**.

**Next turn starts when:** the schedule fires, down to a one-minute minimum.

**Machine on:** yes, awake and with the Desktop app running. **Session open:** no.

It has what a local session has: your files, your MCP config files and
connectors, and permission prompts you configure per task. This is the row to
pick when the work needs something that only exists on your disk.
