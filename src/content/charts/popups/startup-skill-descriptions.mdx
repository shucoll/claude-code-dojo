One-line descriptions of the skills available, so Claude knows what it can
invoke. The skills themselves stay out of context until one is actually used.

This is progressive disclosure, and it is why a skill library scales where a
`CLAUDE.md` of the same content would not. Installing ten more skills adds ten
more lines here.

A skill marked `disable-model-invocation: true` is not in this list at all
and costs nothing until you invoke it by name. After `/compact` this listing is
rebuilt from your current skills and re-injected, on Claude Code v2.1.304 or
later.
