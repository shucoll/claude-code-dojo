Everything Claude loads before you type: its system prompt, your environment (working directory, platform, git state), your `CLAUDE.md` files, saved memory, and the names of available skills and MCP tools.

It is a small share of the window, and you never see it. It is also the part of the window that **reloads automatically after compaction**, which is exactly why standing rules belong in `CLAUDE.md` rather than in a message you sent an hour ago.
