Not a schedule. `/goal` sets a completion condition, and after every turn a small
fast model reads the conversation and judges whether it holds.

**Next turn starts when:** the previous turn finishes and the evaluator says the
condition is not yet met. The verdict's reason becomes guidance for the next
turn.

**Machine on:** yes. **Session open:** yes.

It stops on three verdicts: met, impossible, or an error you have to fix. Under
the surface it is a session-scoped prompt-based `Stop` hook, which is why it is
unavailable when `disableAllHooks` is set.
